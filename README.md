**Home Assistant enthusiast & open-source developer from Italy 🇮🇹**

I build dashboards, integrations and tools for **Home Assistant**, with a focus on smart homes, energy monitoring, EV charging and automation.

- 📡 IoT, Matter & Zigbee devices
- 🖨️ 3D printing & maker projects

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏠 [Casa Dashboard](https://github.com/fabiovit/casa-dashboard)

A modern and customizable dashboard for **Home Assistant**, designed to provide a clean and intuitive overview of your smart home.

### ⚡ [DAZE Dashboard](https://github.com/fabiovit/daze-dashboard)

A Home Assistant dashboard focused on **EV charging**, power management and DAZE wallbox monitoring.

### 🐾 [PawBook](https://github.com/fabiovit/pawbook)

A Home Assistant project dedicated to **pet management**, health tracking and everyday information.

### ♻️ [CBBO Waste Collection](https://github.com/fabiovit/cbbo-waste-collection)

A Home Assistant integration for **waste collection schedules** and recycling information.
